# Data Catalog

- **Meteorology:** rainfall 1h/3h/6h/12h/24h/48h/72h, intensity, trend, temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, forecast, nowcast.
- **Hydrology:** river level, flow, velocity, reservoir, discharge, thresholds, rate of rise.
- **Radar:** reflectivity, rainfall intensity, storm cell, movement, nowcast.
- **Satellite:** optical/radar imagery, flood extent, surface change, landslide indicators.
- **Terrain/geology:** DEM, elevation, slope, aspect, curvature, drainage, watershed, river, soil, geology, historical landslide.
- **Camera/IoT:** CCTV, river/road/landslide camera, water level, weather station, environmental sensors.
- **Exposure:** population, households, schools, health facilities, critical infrastructure, evacuation points.
- **Transport:** roads, bridges, tunnels, traffic, closures, emergency routes.
- **Resources:** teams, ambulances, rescue vehicles, boats, excavators, drones, shelters, supplies.
- **Crowdsourcing:** photo, video, GPS, text report, road blockage, help request.
