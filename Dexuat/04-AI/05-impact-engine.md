# Impact Engine

Input: Hazard + Exposure + Vulnerability + Accessibility.

Output: affected population, households, buildings, roads, schools, health facilities, critical infrastructure, priority score.

Khung ban đầu: `Priority = Hazard × Exposure × Vulnerability × Accessibility`.

Hỗ trợ current, forecast và scenario. Công thức phải hiệu chuẩn trước production.
