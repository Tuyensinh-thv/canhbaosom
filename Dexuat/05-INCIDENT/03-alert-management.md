# Alert Management

Lifecycle: `DRAFT → APPROVAL → ISSUED → DELIVERING → ACKNOWLEDGED/EXPIRED`.

Alert: id, severity, message, target_area, target_users, issued_at, expires_at, channels, approval, status.

Controls: deduplication, escalation, expiration, rate limit, delivery tracking, acknowledgement. Cảnh báo cấp cao tuân thủ quy trình phê duyệt.
