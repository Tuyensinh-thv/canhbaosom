# Incident Management

Lifecycle: `DETECTED → VERIFIED → ACTIVE → RESPONDING → RESOLVED → CLOSED`.

Fields: id, type, severity, location, detected_at, verified_at, status, source, confidence, impact_id, commander, created_by, updated_at.

Nhiều evidence cùng không gian/thời gian có thể correlation thành một incident. Mọi status change có history.
